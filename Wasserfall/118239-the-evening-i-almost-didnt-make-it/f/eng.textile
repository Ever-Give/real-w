The evening I almost didn`t make it

Author: Wasserfall

*000*2025-11-23 13:49

About 1.5 years ago, I was at our local bar with a female friend of mine. Around 10 p.m., we were texting with three other friends (also all female), and they invited us to a pub in the next city. About a 15–20 minute drive away. Spontaneous idea, but sometimes those are the best. We ordered a taxi and agreed to meet them at the city hall.

The taxi arrived quickly. I didn’t stop at the bathroom before leaving. I didn’t really need to go yet, and I didn’t want to keep the driver waiting.

When we got to the city, we still had to wait about ten minutes for our three friends to arrive. That’s when I first noticed a slight pressure building. The walk from the city hall to the pub was only about five minutes. By the time we started walking, my bladder was already quietly complaining.

When we arrived, the queue was enormous, around 40 people. Normally, the pub is packed on weekends, but arriving later than usual meant we were stuck at the very end. There was a small bar outside, so we grabbed a beer to pass the time. Bad decision. I thought fifteen minutes in line would be fine.

Minutes into the queue, the discomfort became urgent. I could feel my bladder screaming. By the time I drank half of the beer I was already desperate. Fifteen minutes into the queue, we had finished our beers. And we were still far from the entrance. People only entered when others left, so progress was agonizingly slow. Every sip and every step made my desperation worse.

After thirty minutes, I confessed to my best friend that I really needed to pee. She asked if I could wait. I said yes, though I wasn’t so sure anymore. Forty-five minutes in, I knew I was reaching my limit. I already started pressing my hands against my crotch whenever nobody was looking, feeling the panic rise.

I whispered to my friend that I had to find a toilet immediately. Otherwise I would pee myself before we are in the pub. Also there was a risk there is a small line for the bathroom, too. My best friend came with me. We rounded the corner next to the pub toward two restaurants but it was past 11 p.m., and both were closed. I always had these restaurants in mind as a last solution while I was waiting in the queue. But I didn't think it was already later this time so I didn't expect that they are closed already. The other pubs are about 5 minutes away in the street next to the city hall. I tried to get there but they also had queues of a few people. So there was no possibilty to find a bathroom soon! And I was at the absolute edge of my bladder’s capacity.

After I realized that the other pubs are no possibilty I even considered walking down to the river (100m) and using the bushes. But there aren't many bushes and you can't really hide between the river and the city walls parallel to the river. Also lots of people meet there on the grass during the day so probably there are still groups of people drinking together outside. My friend tried asking a security guard if we could use the bathroom of a pub, but he refused, probably thinking we were trying to cut the line. The train station with toilets was about 10 minutes away. A very long walk with a bladder close to bursting. What could I do to prevent an accident? I never peed myself in public.

But now I was close to have an accident or at least to lose a spurt. I was really nervous that I would end up in a wet jeans. Restaurants were closed and pubs were crowded. The 2 or 3 pubs we went by had queues long enough that I would maybe lose the battle before reaching the toilet. I knew the city hall directly next to these pubs had a bathroom, but of course the city hall is closed at night so I didn't even try. Shops and shopping centres in Germany are closed at 8 p.m. normally. So they are of course closed, too. Also the fountain behind the city hall didn't help and made me crazy. One moment of inattention and I could lose it!

Then I remembered the parking garage nearby. I knew there were toilets. Two minutes away, but with my bladder screaming at me, I practically sprinted there. Not sprinting but walking as quick as possible without losing a spurt. Every step was agony. The pressure was unbearable. I never felt anything like it in public.

Finally, we arrived. I bolted down the stairs straight to the bathroom. And I made it. What a glorious relief. I emptied my bladder for what felt like an eternity. My friend followed, and we eventually rejoined our three friends at the front of the queue. Finally, we could enter the pub.

From past measurements I know that my bladder can hold more than 1 liter. Who knows if this could have been a new record.

That night, I came dangerously close to peeing myself in the city, in the middle of lots of people. But thank god I made it just in time without even losing a spurt.

Lesson learned: never underestimate the power of a pre-taxi bathroom visit.
